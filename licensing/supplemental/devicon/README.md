`crates/projects/assets/languages/` come from Devicon v2.17.0, revision
`54cfe13ac10eaa1ef817a343ab0a9437eb3c2e08`.

and asset checksums are recorded in that asset directory's `sources.json`;
`licensing/sources.json` pins the license snapshot used by the notice generator.
